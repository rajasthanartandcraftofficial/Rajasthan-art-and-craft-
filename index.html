A Rajasthan origin.

Rajasthan Art And Craft is a Jaipur-based manufacturer, exporter and wholesaler specialising in marble fountains and distinctive stone decor.

Our collection is designed for spaces where material, scale and detail matter — from entrance statements and gardens to luxury interiors and private collections.

We participate in exhibitions across India and are building relationships with international buyers in markets including Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar and the USA.
